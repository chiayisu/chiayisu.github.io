I'm a postdoctoral fellow working with [Maleknaz Nayebi](https://www.maleknazn.ca/) in Lassonde School of Engineering at [York University](https://lassonde.yorku.ca/academics/computer-science). I complete my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at [University of Notre Dame](https://cse.nd.edu/), advised by [Collin McMillan](https://sdf.org/~cmc/) in [Automatic Program Comprehension Lab](https://github.com/apcl-research). My research interests lie in artificial intelligence with the applications to software engineering (AI4SE). Here are some topics that I have explored:
- **Context-aware LLMs for software engineering:** ([ICPC'26 Demo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14434)), ([JSS'25](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0164121225002493)), ([ASE Journal'24](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10515-024-00421-4)), ([ASE Journal'24](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10515-024-00460-x))
- **Programmer-inspired LLMs:** ([Preprint'26](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14535)), ([EMSE'26](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10664-026-10853-z)), ([JSEP'24](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/smr.2706?af=R)), ([ASE'23 NIER](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/10298441))
